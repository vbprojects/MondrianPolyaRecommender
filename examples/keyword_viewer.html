What is this leaf discussing?

YAKE extracts phrases from post text. The disk tree tracks document counts and decayed support as posts move between leaves. Labels describe the retained content; they do not change routing or measure recommendation quality.

Leaf labels

Common = decayed document support. Distinctive = support × positive log-lift against the covered corpus, with add-one smoothing and at least three posts. Repetition within a post counts once.

How to interpret this

Text-derived phrases can label regions with few hashtags. Closely related phrases remain separate: there is no stemming, entity resolution or semantic synonym merging. Unknown-language posts use English extraction; supplied non-English language metadata restricts extraction to hashtags. This fixture lacks language metadata, so its labels need particular care.

Splits move stored keyword contributions with their posts; merges add them at a common time. Neither needs new extraction. Deletions and expiry subtract the old contributions. A leaf with zero distinctive terms is shown honestly rather than assigned a speculative topic.
