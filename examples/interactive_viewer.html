What is the experiment learning?

The model considers possible interest regions in an ancestor-residual tree. It chooses among a fixed pool of real posts by expected information gain: which post's like/dislike response would best distinguish those regions? It is exploring, so it will sometimes show posts it expects you to dislike.

The response model assumes nearby content is more likely to appeal to you. A dislike downweights nearby hypotheses; it does not eliminate a whole branch. Updates are softened to avoid locking in too quickly. These assumptions may not describe your interests.

The bar shows concentration relative to the starting belief, not accuracy or confidence that we know your interests. Skip gives no preference feedback. Text is displayed as archived; no posts are published or liked on Bluesky.
