Decay half-life: 12 ticks. Points fade by age in the decay panels. Density always integrates to one; fading evidence returns predictions toward the uniform prior.

This is a complete-partition Mondrian–Pólya toy. The adaptive policy prunes a seeded random scaffold using an evidence threshold of 8 and a cap of 127 nodes. Rebuilds may change density. The time slider plays Julia-computed snapshots; it does not retrain the model. Scores are independent pre-update mean log densities; higher is better and uniform = 0.
