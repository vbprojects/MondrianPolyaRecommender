Does repeatedly changing latent space damage post representations?

Five synthetic geometries · three seeds · 80 cycles · 384 persistent post UUIDs per run. Every cycle uses fresh data to grow the actual adaptive PPCA tree, then 12 half-lives of silence trigger sibling merging. All policies use the same cuts and model parameters. Original cohort vectors are reserved for evaluation and explicitly labeled refresh/oracle controls.

Lines show means across three seeds; shaded bands show their minimum and maximum, not confidence intervals. Samples are discrete checkpoints. Infinite covariance condition numbers are omitted from plotted lines and remain available in the raw results. Neighbour recall compares compressed-post retrieval against the original vectors, using the same 24 original-vector queries and excluding each query’s own post. Near-complete collapse creates ties, making tiny recall differences unreliable.

NRMSE = reconstruction error divided by the original cohort’s centered spread; 0 is exact and about 1 means error as large as that spread. Routing disagreement is cumulative disagreement with true-vector cut decisions. Effective rank describes the spread of codes within occupied regions, not the accuracy of individual posts. Singular-value flooring limits contraction; orthogonal transport removes it. These deliberately depart from projection and can move a post away from its original embedding. “Refresh” reads original vectors every ten cycles; “remap from initial code” retains the original compressed code and decoder and projects it directly into the current basis.

A well-conditioned map can still erase information

A rank-one basis rotated by angle θ and then mapped back multiplies each code by cos²(θ). Repeating it 100 times gives cos²⁰⁰(θ). Every individual map has condition number 1. Forward projections lose amplitude without requiring an unstable inverse.
