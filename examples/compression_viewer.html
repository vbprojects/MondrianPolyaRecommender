All methods conserve per-source totals for retained buckets. Hourly expiry may remove an observation up to one hour before its exact 24-hour age; use smaller buckets for tighter timing. No source connectors, real text embeddings, named deletion, or deduplication are implemented. Duplicate events are counted as supplied.

Histogram, PPCA, microcluster and replay use the same candidate cuts and pruning rule. Residual splits preserve historical density and grow one level per maintenance. Summary budgets are not byte-matched: compare memory and fidelity together. Scores use independent probes after each update, not prequential evaluation. One seed is a diagnostic, not a quality guarantee. Both trees and summaries remain 2D.

What does “one day” mean for storage?

The compressed density describes activity. A separate archive retains post text, IDs, source, timestamps and vectors for retrieval, corrections and replay.
