All MPPCA variants use the same synthetic proposals. Latent kNN searches all available posts in the generating component’s posterior latent coordinates. The top-5 variant draws one neighbor per proposal to preserve the 32-candidate budget. The full metric also penalizes off-subspace differences. Local actual posts remains a control, not a proposal-snapping method.

Proposal banks contain 64 samples per hypothesis region and are reused. Feedback scales are frozen to the previous experiment. The original-space and actual-post baselines reproduce their previous scores. Repeated posts are excluded after presentation; a draw with no unseen candidates consumes a decision step without feedback. Capped steps assign 61 to failures, not an estimated uncensored stopping time. Actual-post baselines need post IDs; all methods use exact vectors for this offline retrieval/evaluation.

Models were previously fitted on this corpus. Target post IDs are excluded from retrieval, but their vectors influenced training; this is a transductive simulation, not unseen-user validation. A single fixed hidden interest and a correctly specified response likelihood favor localization. No human preference claim follows.
