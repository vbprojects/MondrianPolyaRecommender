100 fixed beliefs over 64 held-out user locations, 32 proposals per belief. Rejected/failed proposal regions receive no substitute. Accepted samples are mapped to original training posts, then scored using a common chord-distance response model. Bootstrap intervals are conditional on this one tree and sample bank.

Conditional moment formulas apply to an untruncated parent Gaussian and a single new cut. They do not exactly account for all inherited box constraints. Rejection sampling respects the final box but the probability-mass transfer remains approximate. Rank-eight subspaces are not necessarily better than rank four for sampling.
