Micro-diagonal is a controlled microcluster-style ablation: same means, mixture weights and per-coordinate variances as MPPCA, with correlations removed. It is not the earlier independently fitted microcluster model. Both Gaussian methods use identical owner regions and clipping rules.

Proposal banks contain 64 samples per hypothesis region and are reused. Repeated posts are excluded after presentation; a draw with no unseen candidates consumes a decision step without feedback. Capped steps assign 61 to failures, not an estimated uncensored stopping time. Actual-post baselines need post IDs; all methods use exact vectors for this offline retrieval/evaluation.

Models were previously fitted on this corpus. Target post IDs are excluded from retrieval, but their vectors influenced training; this is a transductive simulation, not unseen-user validation. A single fixed hidden interest and a correctly specified response likelihood favor localization. No human preference claim follows.
